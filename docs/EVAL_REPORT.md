## Known failure modes

- **These scores do not show generalisation.** The mock's rules and templates were written with these eight tickets in view, so a perfect score shows the pipeline and its safety checks work, not that triage would be accurate on new tickets.
- **Keyword triage.** The mock and `TicketSignals` match English wording. "Not damaged" counts as damage, and a reworded safety hazard is missed.
- **Pattern-based guardrails.** A paraphrased or non-English attack can pass the input scan and the output filter. A sentence with a condition passes as hedged, as in "If you can wait, I will issue a coupon". Actions do not rely on the scan: the catalog, the policy conditions and human approval still apply.
- **Loose retrieval.** BM25 over OR terms matches broadly, so an off-topic document can reach a hosted model. "No relevant document" only means no search hits.
- **Citation checks.** They prove a document was retrieved and safe, not that it supports each sentence of the reply.
- **Answer requirement checks.** They are keyword assertions. They catch missing or forbidden wording, not poor replies.
- **Hosted models.** Unless the header above names one, this report does not measure a hosted model. `LLMAdapter` has only been tested with a fake provider.
- **`eval_008`.** `start_refund_review` is recommended but cannot be executed in this build.
